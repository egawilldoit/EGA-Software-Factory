# Improvement and Metrics

`factory-improve` examines selected, appropriately accessible evidence, distinguishes facts from inferences, identifies recurring failures, compares interventions, and defines validation. It does not automatically rewrite shared skills or collect all sessions.

Outcome summaries are opt-in, local, and versioned. Use null/unknown for missing duration/cost; never fabricate cost. Avoid storing credentials, full prompts, private diffs, or unrelated transcripts. Cross-machine sync is not required for v1. "No improvement justified" is a valid result.
