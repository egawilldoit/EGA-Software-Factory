# Skill Compatibility

Before invoking a candidate skill, verify purpose, dependencies, side effects, stopping point, host, and version. If incompatible, use a safe local fallback and disclose it.

Known snapshot examples (not guarantees):
- Matt implement may include committing work.
- Matt to-spec may publish to an issue tracker.
- Tstack full-send may merge; full-stop has PR/branch behavior.
- Matt code-review may require a comparison point and parallel subagents; fallback assesses specification and standards without claiming independence.

Unavailable or incompatible skills must have a useful fallback. Do not create large vendored dependency trees without need. Respect licenses and attribution. Availability is host-specific.
